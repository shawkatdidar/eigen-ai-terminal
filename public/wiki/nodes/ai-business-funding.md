# AI Business & Funding

## Current State

The AI IPO wave crossed its first definitive threshold: **Anthropic filed a confidential S-1 draft with the SEC on June 1, 2026** (primary source: @AnthropicAI official tweet), confirming the IPO path that had been reported for months. SpaceX S1 filed ($1.75T valuation, May 22); Cerebras IPO closed at $95B (+68% above issue); Anthropic reportedly raising $65B Series H at ~$965B [UNVERIFIED]. Anthropic is consolidating its position: acquired Stainless API (internalizing all SDK/MCP tooling, May 18); hired Andrej Karpathy [UNVERIFIED]; overtook OpenAI as #1 paid business model per Ramp AI Index (May 21); confirmed paying $1B+/month for SpaceX Colossus compute (S1-disclosed). The contrarian signal: Benedict Evans (June 1) argues frontier labs are undifferentiated commodity providers lacking distribution moats (Google/Apple/Meta own those) — margin compression is the predicted trajectory, conflicting with current $965B valuations. New lab formation continues: Recursive AI ($650M, Google Ventures, RSI focus); River AI ($1B target, xAI co-founder [UNVERIFIED]). OpenAI cleared a major legal obstacle: Musk lawsuit dismissed (May 20), strengthening IPO path. OpenRouter ($113M, Capital G/NVIDIA, 12T tokens/month) signals infrastructure-layer conviction in multi-model routing over single-vendor lock-in.
