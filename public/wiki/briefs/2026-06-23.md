## Today I Learned

### Two regimes for restricting frontier-model access: capability-gating vs. nationality-gating
**Why this came up:** OpenAI's "Trusted Access for Cyber" gating of GPT-5.5-Cyber, alongside Anthropic's Glasswing/Mythos trusted-access — and the June 13 export-control directive.

There are now two distinct mechanisms restricting who can use a frontier model, and they answer different questions:

1. **Capability-gating (trusted access).** The restriction is keyed to the *model's capability* and the *user's vetting status*. A cyber-offense-capable model is released only to "trusted defenders" / vetted partners who pass a screening program. The lab decides who is trustworthy. Examples: Anthropic Glasswing/Mythos "trusted access," OpenAI "Trusted Access for Cyber." The goal is to capture the defensive value of a dual-use capability while limiting offensive misuse — *self-imposed* by the lab.

2. **Nationality-gating (export control).** The restriction is keyed to the *user's nationality/jurisdiction*, imposed by a *government*. The June 13 directive suspended Fable 5 / Mythos 5 for all foreign nationals worldwide. The model's specific capability matters only insofar as it crosses a regulatory threshold; the gate is about *where you are / what passport you hold*, not whether you're individually trusted.

The judgment payoff: when you read "Model X has restricted access," ask **which regime** — because they have opposite failure modes and opposite beneficiaries. Capability-gating concentrates power in the lab (the lab is the trust authority, and can use "trust" to favor partners); its failure mode is regulatory capture and opacity ("who got vetted, and why?"). Nationality-gating concentrates power in the state; its failure mode is fragmenting the global research ecosystem along borders and pushing excluded users toward open-weight substitutes. A single model (Mythos) is now under *both* regimes simultaneously — gated by Anthropic *and* export-controlled by the US — which is why "model access as a strategic input" became this month's dominant theme.

### Quick Glossary
| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Trusted access / capability-gating | Lab-imposed restriction: a high-capability (often dual-use) model is released only to vetted/screened users. The lab is the trust authority. |
| Export control / nationality-gating | Government-imposed restriction keyed to the user's nationality/jurisdiction, not individual trust. |
| Dual-use | A capability useful for both defense and offense (here: finding/exploiting software vulnerabilities). Drives the gating logic. |
| Inpainting | Filling in missing or masked regions of an image so the result looks coherent — an image-editing task distinct from generation-from-scratch. |
| OCR | Optical character recognition — extracting machine-readable text from images/scanned documents. |
