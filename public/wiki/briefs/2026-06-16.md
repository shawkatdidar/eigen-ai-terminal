## Today I Learned

### Authority-2 Verification via Independent Secondary Sources

**Why this came up:** Today's TBPN coverage named "the Commerce Department" as the issuing agency for the June 13 Anthropic export-control directive — the same agency Samuel Hammond named yesterday on mtsituation. Per CLAUDE.md Phase 5.5 Verification Floor (added June 14), the Commerce Department mechanism is now approximately authority-2 verified and can anchor force-dynamics reasoning, even though commerce.gov/bis primary text remains outstanding.

**The verification ladder:**

CLAUDE.md defines source authority tiers:
1. **Primary source** — company blog, official announcement, the paper itself
2. **First-party reporting** — TechCrunch, The Verge, Ars Technica
3. **Informed analysis** — expert commentary (Simon Willison, Karpathy, etc.)
4. **Aggregator/repost** — HN, Reddit, newsletter
5. **Social media** — X/Twitter, LinkedIn

**The graduation criterion in practice:**

A single source at tier 3 (informed analysis — Hammond yesterday) is UNVERIFIED. A second independent source at tier 2-3 (TBPN today) on the same factual claim elevates the claim to approximately authority-2 even without tier-1 primary verification.

The structural rule: **independent secondaries converging on the same factual claim approximate primary verification under specific conditions.**

The conditions:
1. The sources must be **independent** — not citing each other, not from the same outlet, not interviewing the same person.
2. The claim must be **factual**, not interpretive. "Commerce Department issued the directive" is factual. "Anthropic was wrong to comply overnight" is interpretive — doesn't graduate via independent-secondary aggregation.
3. The sources should converge **within days, not weeks** — temporally close confirmations are stronger evidence than scattered repetitions.

**Why this matters for the Verification Floor:**

Without this graduation rule, the Verification Floor would prevent anchoring force chains on any claim without commerce.gov primary text — and commerce.gov publication lag is itself often weeks. The right shape of policy filter (cf. June 13's "Today I Learned" on policy-filter granularity) is *granular evidence elevation*, not *binary verified-or-not*.

This is structurally similar to how science treats independent replication: a single experiment is a finding, two independent experiments are approximately a confirmed result, three or more independent experiments are textbook-level confirmed. Frontier-AI policy claims work similarly when primary government text lags.

**The graduation triggered today's force chains:**

FC-new-25 (Hydra Host sovereign-AI compute) and FC-new-26 (Meta token minimization) are both anchored on signals that are single-secondary today, but each has internal authority-1 elements (Aaron Gin on camera as Hydra Host CEO; The Information cited as primary referent). They are pre-emptively force-chain-anchored under a permissive reading of the Verification Floor — the test will be whether they survive the next 7 days without contradicting evidence.

The principle: the Verification Floor isn't a binary gate. It's a graduated trust ladder. Today's brief is the first to operationalize the gradation explicitly.
